About Me

Documentation isn't just a nice-to-have—it's the difference between product success and user frustration. I create documentation that bridges the gap between complex products and the people who use them.

As a Technical Writer specializing in product documentation and knowledge management, I combine traditional expertise with modern approaches: Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) frameworks and AI-powered workflows. But I don't just document features—I document solutions to user problems. That's why I use JTBD: to understand what users are really trying to accomplish.

I simplify complex concepts, create engaging visuals, and adapt quickly to new tools and processes. I'm also deep into AI-powered documentation workflows—both using them myself and helping teams adopt them. The combination of JTBD thinking + AI tools creates documentation that's faster to produce and more valuable to users.

Throughout my career, I've gained hands-on experience in technical documentation, knowledge management, content management systems, and end-to-end product journeys. My toolkit includes everything from traditional platforms (Confluence, OxygenXML) to modern systems (Document360, Componize) to AI assistants—all focused on one goal: making products easier to understand and use.
